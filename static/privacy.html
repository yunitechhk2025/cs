我们收集哪些信息

  • 您发送的消息内容：您在聊天中输入的文字问题，以及您主动发送的图片。

我们如何使用这些信息

  • 理解您的问题并生成回答；
  • 在 AI 无法确定答案时，转交人工客服跟进处理；
  • 统计常见问题，改进客服知识库的准确性。

我们不会将您的信息用于广告投放，也不会出售给第三方。

  • Meta Platforms：使用 WhatsApp 渠道时，消息经由 WhatsApp 商业平台传输，适用 Meta 的隐私政策。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026 · Operated by Yuni Tech Limited

This page explains how Yuni Tech Limited ("we") collects, uses and protects your information when providing our AI customer service, available through our web chat widget and our WhatsApp business number (+852 5361 5061).

Information we collect

  • Message content you send us, including text questions and any images you choose to share.
  • WhatsApp account details: your WhatsApp phone number and public profile name when you contact us on WhatsApp.
  • Conversation history, so a human agent has the context needed to follow up.

We never ask for identity documents, payment card details or passwords. Please do not share them in chat.

How we use it

  • To understand your question and generate an answer;
  • To hand the conversation to a human agent when the AI is not confident;
  • To identify common questions and improve the accuracy of our knowledge base.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it to third parties.

Who we share it with

  • Meta Platforms, when you reach us over WhatsApp; Meta's own privacy policy applies to that transport.
  • Our AI model provider, which receives your question text (and text descriptions of images) solely to generate a reply.
  • Our support agents, who can read a conversation once it is escalated to them.

Retention

Conversation records are stored on our servers for service quality and knowledge base improvement. You may contact us at any time to request deletion of records relating to you.
